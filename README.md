# Tribute Page — Ada Lovelace

A single-page tribute to Ada Lovelace, widely regarded as the world's
first computer programmer, built as personal practice for the OIBSIP
Web Development internship (Level 2, Task 2).

## Live Preview
Open `index.html` in any browser — no build step required.

## Sections
- Hero — portrait, name, and tagline
- Biography — 4 original paragraphs covering her life, her work with
  Charles Babbage, and her contributions to computing theory
- Timeline — key milestones from her birth to the naming of the Ada
  programming language in her honor
- Quote — her well-known 1843 quote on the Analytical Engine
- Footer

## Design decisions
- **Typography:** Serif headings (Georgia) paired with sans-serif
  body text (Arial) — the serif gives a classical, period-appropriate
  feel fitting a 19th-century subject, while sans-serif keeps the
  actual reading content clean and modern.
- **Color palette:** Warm, muted historical tones (deep brown, cream,
  light tan) alternating across sections for visual separation,
  rather than a bright modern brand palette.
- **Timeline cards:** Styled with a colored left border and rounded
  corners, rather than a plain bulleted list, to give the milestones
  section real visual structure.

## Sourcing
- Portrait sourced from Wikimedia Commons (public domain).
- Biographical content is original writing based on well-documented
  historical facts about Ada Lovelace's life and work.
- The featured quote is from Ada Lovelace's own 1843 notes, in the
  public domain.
